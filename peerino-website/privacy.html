Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary

Peerino is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect personal data, track usage, or send your files to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, IP address logs)
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies for profiling or advertising
  • No files: on LAN and STUN, transfers are direct between peers. When a direct connection is impossible, a TURN relay (Cloudflare) forwards the encrypted traffic.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Peer ID: a randomly generated identifier stored in config/peer_id.json
  • Shared files: stored in your local shared-folder/
  • Configuration: stored in config/. A .env file is used only during development or for manual overrides; it is not required for the released app.

All this data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the Peerino folder.

Third-Party Services

Peerino uses the following external services only for connection setup (never for file content):

  • Signaling: 0.peerjs.com sees temporary connection metadata (Peer IDs and public IP addresses for ICE negotiation), not file content.
  • STUN servers: public servers (Google, Cloudflare, etc.) used to discover your public IP for direct connections.
  • TURN server (Cloudflare, if a direct connection is impossible): Cloudflare TURN relays the encrypted traffic when a direct peer-to-peer connection cannot be established (e.g. symmetric NAT, mobile networks). The relay sees encrypted traffic only and cannot decrypt it. Peerino does not operate any file storage servers.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to request, export, or delete. You have full control over your local data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect the change.
